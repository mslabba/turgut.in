AI Agent Development: Agents That Do Real Work

We design, build and deploy AI agents that work inside your business systems. They query live data, call your tools and APIs, and hand off to people when a decision needs a human. We run agents like these in our own products and operations every day.

  • check_circleScoped roles: one job per agent, with only the tools and permissions that job needs
  • check_circleLive data, not guesses: answers come from your systems through secure queries and tools
  • check_circleRole-based access: sensitive records are only visible to authorized users
  • check_circleHuman handoff: escalation paths are defined up front, and people make the final calls
  • check_circleReview and iterate: we audit transcripts and outputs, then refine prompts, knowledge and guardrails

Frequently Asked Questions

How is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

add

A chatbot answers questions. An AI agent also takes steps in your systems: it queries live data, calls tools and APIs, updates records and hands work to a person when a decision needs a human. ConnectEdu's Admin AI Agent, for example, answers from live school ERP data through secure database queries and can add or update students and staff.

How do you keep people in control of what an agent does?

add

We design approval gates into the workflow. Agents prepare and execute inside clear boundaries, and a person decides what goes live, what gets sent and what needs review. We also scope each agent's tools and permissions to its job instead of giving one agent access to everything.

Do you build voice agents?

add

Yes. We have used xAI's Voice Agent Builder to run first-touch outreach calls for ConnectEdu. The agent introduces the product, asks qualification questions and routes qualified leads to a human salesperson. We wrote up the experiment on our blog.

Which AI models and frameworks do you work with?

add

We choose models per use case. Our stack includes OpenAI, Claude, Gemini and xAI Grok models, plus LangGraph and the Model Context Protocol (MCP) for tool connections.

Can you add AI agents to an existing SaaS product?

add

Yes. We connect agents to your existing database, APIs and tools, and keep AI workloads separate from core production requests so model latency doesn't slow your application down. Our SaaS product engineering service covers the product side.
