PowerAuction: Sports Player Auction Software

PowerAuction runs sports player auctions from the first registration link to the last sold player. It covers registration, payments, team purses, live bidding and analytics, built for cricket, football and multi-sport leagues.

Visit thepowerauction.com open_in_newTalk to Us About Your League calendar_today

Key Capabilities

One platform for organizers, players, team owners and auction operators.

how_to_reg

Player Registration

Custom registration forms with sport-specific fields, shared by link over WhatsApp, email or social media. Optional fees are collected through the payment gateway, with paid and pending status for each player.

category

Categories & Base Prices

Group players into categories such as Icon, Overseas, Local or Emerging, or define your own, each with its own base price. You can also run an open auction.

groups

Teams & Purses

Create franchises with logos and owners, set purse values and squad rules, and track spend and remaining budget as players are sold.

gavel

Live Auction Control

Player cards with stats, base price, current bid and countdown, plus random next-player selection. Public boards and display screens update in real time.

dashboard

Team Owner Dashboards

A secure real-time link for each franchise showing squad, spend, remaining purse and the player currently on the block.

insights

Sponsors & Analytics

Show sponsor logos on auction screens. After the event, review sold players, team spend and category distribution, and export reports.

How It Works

Set up before auction day, run it live, and review the results afterwards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is sports player auction software?

add

It helps league organizers run the whole player auction: registration and payments, team purses, live bidding and post-event analytics. That replaces spreadsheets, paper paddles and chat threads.

Can I run a cricket player auction with PowerAuction?

add

Yes. PowerAuction is used for cricket league and franchise-style player auctions, with configurable categories, squad rules and purse tracking.

Which sports does PowerAuction support?

add

It is multi-sport. Cricket, football, futsal, basketball, volleyball, and corporate or school/college formats can all use the same auction workflow with sport-specific setup.

Can team owners track their remaining purse during the auction?

add

Yes. Each team can have a secure real-time dashboard showing purse, amount spent, remaining budget, acquired players and the player currently on the block.

Can players register and pay registration fees online?

add

Yes. Organizers share a registration link by WhatsApp, email, social media or a direct URL. Players complete their profile on any device and can pay an optional registration fee through the payment gateway.
