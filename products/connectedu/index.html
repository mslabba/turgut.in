ConnectEdu: AI School ERP & Parent Engagement Platform

ConnectEdu connects communication, attendance, fees, transportation, examinations and academics in one platform for schools. Its built-in Admin AI Agent answers administrators' questions from live school data.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is ConnectEdu?

add

ConnectEdu is a school ERP and parent engagement platform built by InRay Labs. It brings communication, attendance, fees, transportation, examinations and academics into one platform, and includes an Admin AI Agent for school administrators.

What can the ConnectEdu Admin AI Agent do?

add

It answers administrators' questions from live school ERP data using secure database queries, covering fees, attendance, student counts, fee collection, staff, transport and exams. It can also take actions such as adding and updating students and staff.

Can staff ask the Admin AI Agent questions in their own language?

add

Yes. Staff can ask in any language, and the agent answers in English.

Does ConnectEdu support biometric attendance and online fee payments?

add

Yes. ConnectEdu connects with fingerprint, RFID card and facial recognition attendance devices and keeps parents informed. Parents can pay fees online by card, UPI or net banking, with automated reminders and receipts.

How can my school get a ConnectEdu demo?

add

Book a school demo through our Calendly demo link, or visit connectedu.in to learn more.
