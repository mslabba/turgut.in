Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 29 July 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how InRay Labs ("we", "our", or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, remember your preferences, analyse website traffic, and improve user experience.

2 How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • check_circle Ensure website functions correctly
  • check_circle Remember user preferences
  • check_circle Measure website performance
  • check_circle Analyse visitor behaviour
  • check_circle Improve products & services
  • check_circle Enhance website security

3 Types of Cookies We Use

verified_user Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They cannot be switched off.

Examples include:

  • Session management
  • Security
  • Form submission
  • Load balancing

analytics Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

Examples include:

  • Number of visitors
  • Pages visited
  • Traffic sources
  • Time spent on pages

We may use services such as Google Analytics for this purpose.

settings_suggest Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language settings or previously entered information, to improve your browsing experience.

ads_click Marketing Cookies

Where applicable, marketing cookies may be used to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns and deliver more relevant content.

4 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies may be placed by trusted third-party services integrated into our website, such as:

insights Google Analytics
map Google Maps
smart_display YouTube
work LinkedIn
share Meta (Facebook)
extension Other embedded services

These providers may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

5 Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to:

  • visibility View stored cookies
  • delete_forever Delete cookies
  • block Block cookies
  • tune Restrict cookies
  • notifications_active Receive notifications before cookies are stored

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

6 Changes to This Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our business practices. Updates will be posted on this page.

7 Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Cookie Policy, please contact us using the contact information provided on our website.
